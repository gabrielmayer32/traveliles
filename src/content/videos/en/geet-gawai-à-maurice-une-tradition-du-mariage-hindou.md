Among the traditions brought to Mauritius by Indian immigrants, Geet Gawai holds a special place. This colourful pre-wedding ceremony brings together rituals, prayers, songs, music and dance in an atmosphere that is both spiritual and celebratory.

Passed down through generations within Bhojpuri-speaking communities, whose roots lie mainly in northern India and the state of Bihar, Geet Gawai is traditionally led by the women of the family and neighbourhood. Through their songs, gestures and rhythms, they celebrate the bride and groom while keeping alive a cultural memory deeply woven into Mauritian history.

Recognised by UNESCO as part of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, Geet Gawai reflects the richness of the traditions that have shaped the island’s identity.
