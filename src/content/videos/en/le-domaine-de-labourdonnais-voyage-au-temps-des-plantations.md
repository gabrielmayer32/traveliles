In Mapou, in the north of Mauritius, the Domaine de Labourdonnais invites visitors to step back in time and experience the refined lifestyle of 19th-century Mauritius.

At the heart of this former sugar estate stands the Château de Labourdonnais, an elegant neoclassical colonial mansion surrounded by expansive gardens. Carefully restored, the château reveals its distinctive architecture, period furnishings and the stories of the people who shaped the estate over generations.

The visit continues through the tropical orchards, where mango, lychee and other fruit trees thrive, before leading to the distillery and the spaces dedicated to products made on the estate.

Blending heritage, nature and local craftsmanship, the Domaine de Labourdonnais offers a captivating insight into a lesser-known chapter of Mauritian history. It is a timeless journey through one of the island’s most emblematic heritage sites, where the legacy of the past remains very much alive.
