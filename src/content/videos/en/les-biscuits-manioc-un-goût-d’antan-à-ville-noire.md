For nearly 150 years, the H. Rault Biscuit Factory in Ville Noire has preserved one of Mauritius’ most distinctive culinary traditions: the handmade production of cassava biscuits.

The story began with Hilarion Rault, who set out to recreate traditional Breton biscuits using ingredients available locally. His experiments led to a unique cassava-based recipe that gradually became part of Mauritius’ culinary heritage.

The original production method is still followed today. Inside this charming, small-scale factory, every stage reflects skills passed down through generations and a deep respect for traditional craftsmanship.

Travel-Îles steps inside the H. Rault Biscuit Factory to explore the story behind these iconic Mauritian cassava biscuits, whose delicate texture and distinctive flavours evoke the island’s past.

A delicious journey into a place where time seems to have stood still, and where one of Mauritius’ most treasured recipes continues to live on.
