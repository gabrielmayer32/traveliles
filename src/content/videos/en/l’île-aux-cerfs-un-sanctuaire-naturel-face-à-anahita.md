Just across the lagoon from Anahita Golf & Spa Resort, Île aux Cerfs offers residents and guests easy access to one of Mauritius’ most iconic island destinations.

Famous for its white-sand beaches and turquoise waters, the island also offers a wide range of activities. Golf enthusiasts can enjoy its celebrated course, while more adventurous visitors can explore the island from above on a treetop adventure course set amid the natural surroundings.

A dedicated beach area has also been created exclusively for Anahita residents and guests. With beach service, dining facilities and a beautifully preserved setting, it provides the perfect place to unwind, swim, enjoy lunch with your feet in the sand or simply take in the views across the lagoon.

Located just off the Anahita estate, Île aux Cerfs becomes a natural extension of the resort experience: a privileged escape combining nature, leisure and the relaxed rhythm of island life in Mauritius.
