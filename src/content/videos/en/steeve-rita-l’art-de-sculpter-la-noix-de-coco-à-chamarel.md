You may already have spotted him at the Chamarel viewpoint, carefully transforming a coconut shell into a one-of-a-kind creation.

Steeve Rita is a Mauritian sculptor with a distinctive talent. With patience, precision and imagination, he gives new life to one of the island’s most familiar natural materials. In his hands, coconut shells become decorative objects, handcrafted souvenirs and original pieces shaped entirely by hand.

Take a moment to stop and watch him at work. Steeve is always happy to share his skills and introduce visitors to the many creations he produces with passion. It is a simple, authentic encounter set against the striking landscapes of Chamarel, and a chance to meet one of the artisans keeping Mauritian craftsmanship and creativity alive.

Through Steeve’s story and his meticulous gestures, Travel-Îles invites you to discover another side of Mauritius: an island of talented people, natural materials and artisans who transform them with care, imagination and heart.
