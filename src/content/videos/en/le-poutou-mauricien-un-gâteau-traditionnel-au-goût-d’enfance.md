“Enn poutou so, avek so diber ek enn bon dite” — a few simple words are enough to bring back childhood memories for many Mauritians.

Made primarily from rice, poutou is a traditional Mauritian cake loved for its soft texture and comforting simplicity. With roots in Indian cuisine, it has gradually become part of Mauritius’ culinary heritage and remains one of the island’s most nostalgic childhood treats.

Served warm with a little butter and a cup of tea, poutou recalls after-school snacks, family gatherings and the simple pleasures of days gone by. Its familiar taste has been passed down from one generation to the next.

More than a traditional Mauritian sweet, poutou carries a sense of shared memory, shaped by familiar flavours, family recipes and moments enjoyed together.

Travel-Îles revisits this much-loved comfort food, a humble delicacy that continues to evoke the warmth and authenticity of Mauritian childhood.
