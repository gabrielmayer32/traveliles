They spin flames through the darkness and captivate audiences with every performance. The artists of Fire Tribe Mauritius have turned fire twirling into a powerful form of expression.

Behind the intensity and beauty of each show lie countless hours of training, precise technique and strict safety procedures. Working with fire leaves no room for improvisation. Focus, coordination and trust are essential to transform this unpredictable element into a striking live performance.

The members of Fire Tribe Mauritius share the passion, discipline and motivation that drive them, as well as the unique connection they have developed with fire. Their craft sits at the crossroads of dance, performance and visual art, combining movement, rhythm and spectacle.

Travel-Îles meets a talented group of Mauritian fire performers whose skill and energy never fail to leave a lasting impression.
