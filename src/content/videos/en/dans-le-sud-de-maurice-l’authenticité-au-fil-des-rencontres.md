Some places invite you to slow down, connect with others and rediscover traditions that have stood the test of time. In southern Mauritius, between Souillac and Chamouny, everyday encounters reveal a region where community spirit, traditional skills and a close connection to nature remain an important part of life.

### Souillac: Where Community Spirit Comes Naturally

In Souillac, the peaceful pace of village life goes hand in hand with the warmth of its people. Here, different backgrounds and cultures come together naturally, united by a strong sense of belonging. "Outside, we are all one." A few simple words from a local resident that capture the spirit of solidarity and togetherness so deeply rooted in Mauritian life.

### Chamouny: Keeping Traditional Skills Alive

Further south, at the Sensitive Garden in Chamouny, nature is both a source of inspiration and a place to learn. Here, traditional skills are kept alive through the use of natural materials, from coconut fibres to leaves woven into simple, practical objects. A handwoven hat, a leaf used as a natural tie… Small gestures that speak of a time when people made the most of what nature provided, wasting little and relying on resourcefulness.

The same philosophy extends to the concept of a _food forest_, where what grows in the garden finds its way directly onto the plate. It is a way of living that celebrates fresh ingredients, seasonal produce and a closer connection to the land. And, of course, there is the familiar pleasure of freshly pressed sugarcane juice.

Through these encounters, another side of southern Mauritius comes to life. One where traditions are more than memories of the past. They remain part of everyday life, carried forward by the people who continue to practise and share them. A journey through the South, discovering the people, traditions and simple pleasures that give this part of Mauritius its distinctive character.
