As night falls, Desforges Street, officially SSR Street, in Plaine Verte takes on a whole new character. Food stalls open, the scent of spices fills the air and crowds gather around the many vendors for a quick and flavourful meal.

One of the best-known street food spots in Port Louis, the street transforms every evening into a lively open-air food court. Local residents, workers, families and visitors come together to enjoy generous, affordable dishes that are deeply rooted in everyday Mauritian life.

From grilled specialities and stuffed breads to noodles, spicy dishes and local sweets, Desforges Street reveals a vibrant, welcoming and distinctly food-loving side of the capital.

Travel-Îles takes you into the heart of Plaine Verte, along one of those streets that never truly sleeps, where food tells the story of Mauritius’ cultural diversity, one dish at a time.
