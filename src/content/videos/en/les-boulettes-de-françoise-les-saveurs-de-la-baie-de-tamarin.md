At Tamarin Bay, enjoying a bowl of boulettes comes with more than just a sea view. There’s a special atmosphere here, one that Françoise Ricot has been part of for nearly twenty years. Her small food business has become much more than a place to grab a bite: it’s a familiar meeting spot where locals and visitors come together.

Rooted in Mauritius’ Sino-Mauritian culinary heritage, boulettes (steamed dumplings) are among the island’s most beloved specialities. Served hot in a flavourful broth, they are one of those simple pleasures that can be enjoyed at almost any time of day.

**A Family Venture That Became a Tradition**

For Françoise, it all began with a desire to work for herself and build something of her own. Encouraged by her husband, she first learned the trade alongside her father-in-law before gradually setting up her own business.

Almost two decades later, Françoise and her husband are still a familiar presence at Tamarin Bay, where their food stall has become part of everyday life. Here, people know one another, stop for a chat and gather over a steaming bowl of boulettes or noodles (the famous “_mines_”).

**The Taste of Tamarin**

Beyond the food itself, it is the atmosphere of the bay that makes the experience so special. This is a place where generations meet, where people come to enjoy the beach, catch up with friends and share a tasty, affordable meal in a relaxed setting.

Through Françoise’s journey, we discover another side of Mauritius: one shaped by small family-run businesses, culinary traditions passed down through generations and the spontaneous encounters that give local life its unmistakable charm.
