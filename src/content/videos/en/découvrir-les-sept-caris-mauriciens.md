To truly understand a country, you need to meet its people, explore its traditions and taste its food. In Mauritius, every flavour tells a story of cultural diversity, family heritage and generosity.

Located in Carreau Laliane, Le Grand Désir welcomes both visitors and Mauritians for an authentic and convivial dining experience. At the heart of the meal are the seven curries, a generous feast that showcases the richness and diversity of Mauritian cuisine.

More than simply lunch, the experience is an invitation to slow down, share stories and gain a deeper understanding of the island through its family recipes and culinary traditions.

Travel-Îles takes you to Le Grand Désir, where every dish celebrates the joy of sharing and Mauritius’ vibrant food heritage. Expect a warm, genuine and flavourful experience that offers a true taste of the island.
