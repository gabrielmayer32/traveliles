A new chapter begins for Côte Nord magazine. The publication is evolving into Travel-Îles by Côte Nord, with a renewed ambition: to reveal Mauritius in a different way, through its places, traditions, flavours and, above all, the people who bring the island to life.

This new identity builds on Côte Nord’s editorial heritage while broadening its perspective to include Mauritius and the wider Indian Ocean region. Stories, encounters, discoveries and experiences will offer a closer look at a vibrant, authentic and richly diverse part of the world.

The new Travel-Îles by Côte Nord magazine will soon be available from newsstands across Mauritius. A digital edition will also be offered through our website, with subscription access allowing readers to enjoy every new issue wherever they are.

A new journey begins, driven by the same passion for islands, their stories and the people at their heart.
