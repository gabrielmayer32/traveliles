In April 1994, Côte Nord Magazine also devoted a full page to Paradise Cove, newly renovated at the time and already receiving several distinctions that reflected its growing ambitions.

The Anse La Raie hotel had recently joined Small Luxury Hotels of the World, which at the time brought together only 104 hotels worldwide. The article also mentioned the _Fleurir Maurice_ award and its selection by the French _Passe-Privilège_ label. Renovated rooms, redesigned beaches, a broader dining offer and enhanced services all contributed to the hotel’s move further upmarket.

Beyond the accolades, the article already highlighted what made the property distinctive: its small coves, lagoon, lush vegetation, intimate atmosphere and views towards the northern islands.

More than thirty years later, this archive captures a period when Mauritian hospitality was further strengthening its reputation internationally while continuing to showcase what made it unique: the warmth of Mauritian hospitality.

(Côte Nord Magazine, Issue No. 1, April 1994.)
