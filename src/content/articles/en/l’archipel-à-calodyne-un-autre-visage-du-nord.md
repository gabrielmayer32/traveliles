In April 1994, in the very first issue of Côte Nord Magazine, Calodyne was already presented as a peaceful alternative to the growing bustle of Grand Baie and Pereybère.

It was in this setting that the magazine introduced L’Archipel, a small, newly renovated hotel complex comprising nine bungalows, four studios and one apartment. A swimming pool overlooking the sea, views of the northern islands, carefully prepared cuisine, personalised service and sega evenings: the property embraced an intimate style of hospitality, deeply rooted in the Mauritian way of life.

Reading this article more than thirty years later also takes us back to a time when tourism development in the North was still shaped by small, independent and family-run establishments. It offers a snapshot of Calodyne before the major transformations of the coastline, when tranquillity, tropical surroundings and warm hospitality were already among its greatest attractions.

(Côte Nord Magazine, Issue No. 1, April 1994)
