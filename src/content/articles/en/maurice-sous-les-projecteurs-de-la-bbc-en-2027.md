**Beyond its beaches and lagoons, the people, cultural richness and natural heritage of Mauritius are set to reach audiences around the world. The Mauritius Tourism Promotion Authority (MTPA) has entered into a commercial partnership with BBC Studios, opening up new opportunities to showcase the destination internationally.**

Signed at the World Travel Market in London, held from 3 to 5 November 2026, the agreement will pave the way for a global campaign in 2027, featuring two complementary productions: a special edition of _The Travel Show_ and a three-part series entitled _The Art of Escape_.

Through this partnership, the MTPA is embracing a destination marketing strategy centred on immersive travel, authentic encounters and meaningful experiences. While Mauritius’ iconic landscapes continue to inspire travellers, the island has much more to offer international visitors.

Its cultural diversity, culinary traditions, local craftsmanship, biodiversity and wealth of experiences invite travellers to connect with the destination on a more personal level.

By partnering with BBC Studios, Mauritius will also benefit from international exposure, reaching audiences eager to explore the island from a fresh perspective.

**The Travel Show: A Journey into the Heart of Mauritius**

<figure><img src="/images/uploads/BBC%20Travel%20Show%20Mozambique%203.webp" alt=""><figcaption>Emeline Nsingi Nkosi in Mozambique - BBC Travel Show</figcaption></figure>

BBC News’ weekly travel programme, _The Travel Show_, explores destinations through engaging stories, cultural encounters and insights into contemporary travel and tourism. The programme was recognised at the 2026 Travel Media Awards.

The Mauritius special will be hosted by bilingual journalist and presenter Emeline Nsingi Nkosi, who will explore the island’s culinary heritage, diverse landscapes, natural treasures and wildlife conservation initiatives.

The programme will also be supported by additional digital content on BBC.com and the BBC app, as well as distribution through selected airlines’ in-flight entertainment systems.

<figure><img src="/images/uploads/30-DSCF9112.webp" alt=""><figcaption>Macondé, Mauritius (Lucie Lagesse Mayer©)</figcaption></figure>

**The Art of Escape: Three Journeys of Discovery**

Produced by BBC StoryWorks Commercial Productions, this three-part branded content series will follow travel photographer Jack Swynnerton as he explores some of the lesser-known sides of Mauritius.

The series will focus on three distinct themes: food and culture, outdoor activities and adventure, and nature and wildlife.

It is an opportunity to celebrate what makes Mauritius truly distinctive: spontaneous encounters, living traditions, remarkable natural diversity and an extraordinary range of experiences within a single island.

This approach naturally resonates with the editorial vision of Travel-Îles: a destination is defined not only by the places we see, but also by the experiences we share and the people we meet along the way.

We look forward to seeing Mauritius through these fresh perspectives in 2027.
