More than one million travellers have visited Mauritius since the beginning of 2026. Reaching this milestone as early as September highlights the enduring appeal of this Indian Ocean destination, celebrated not only for its natural beauty but also for its rich cultural heritage and warm hospitality.

According to Statistics Mauritius, the island welcomed 1,042,241 tourists between January and September 2026, an increase of 3.4% compared with the same period in 2025. This encouraging trend follows a record-breaking year in 2025, when Mauritius attracted more than 1.4 million visitors. France remains the island's leading tourism market, with over 226,000 arrivals, while Germany, India and South Africa have also recorded notable growth.

**Beyond the Beaches and Lagoons**

While Mauritius is renowned for its beautiful beaches, turquoise lagoons and pleasant climate, there is so much more to discover beyond its coastline. From the bustling streets of Port Louis and charming coastal villages to tea plantations and the lush landscapes of the South, the island reveals a diversity that goes far beyond its picture-postcard image.

Its cuisine, heritage, traditions and, above all, encounters with local people bring something special to every visit. This cultural richness, combined with a genuine sense of hospitality deeply rooted in Mauritian life, is what makes the island such a distinctive destination.

**Looking Ahead with Optimism**

Following several days of tensions and disruptions, Mauritius is gradually returning to its usual rhythm. As daily activities resume, the island looks towards the coming weeks with renewed calm and optimism. Recent events also serve as a reminder of the importance of preserving what makes Mauritius special: its diversity, solidarity and longstanding tradition of living together in harmony. 

As the island enters the final quarter of 2026, with the possibility of another record-breaking year for tourism, welcoming one million visitors represents far more than a statistical milestone. It reflects the lasting appeal of this small Indian Ocean island, whose true beauty lies not only in its landscapes, but also in the people who give Mauritius its unique character and spirit.
