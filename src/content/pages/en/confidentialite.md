## Data controller

traveliles.com is published by Travel-Îles, in Mauritius. For any question about your personal data: [contact@traveliles.com](mailto:contact@traveliles.com).

Your data is processed in accordance with the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 and, for readers living in the European Union (including Réunion), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Data we collect and why

**Contact form.** First name, last name, email address, country and message, used only to reply to your request. The message is sent through our provider Web3Forms and kept for 12 months at most.

**Newsletter.** Your email address, with your consent, to send you the newsletter. It is managed by Mailchimp and kept until you unsubscribe, which you can do at any time through the link in every email.

**Audience measurement.** Only if you accept, Google Analytics measures site traffic (pages viewed, device type, approximate country). IP addresses are anonymised and data is kept for 14 months at most.

**Embedded videos.** YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook and Instagram players load only after you agree, through the banner or the button on each video. These platforms may then set their own cookies, governed by their privacy policies.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data (IP address, HTTP headers) for security and performance. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Travel-Îles uses no advertising pixels and never sells or shares your data.

## Cookies and local storage

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser (`ti-cookies`, `ti-cookies-date`, `ti-video`). This stays on your device and is not sent to anyone. Your choice is kept for 6 months, after which the banner is shown again.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets `_ga` cookies and video players may set their own cookies. Declining is as easy as accepting, and you can change your choice at any time through the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page.

## Transfers outside Mauritius

Our providers (Cloudflare, Google, Mailchimp, Web3Forms and the video platforms) are located outside Mauritius, notably in the United States. These transfers rely on your consent or on the contractual safeguards offered by these providers, under the conditions of section 36 of the Data Protection Act 2017.

## Security

Access to your data is limited to the people who need it. In the event of a personal data breach, we notify the Data Protection Commissioner within the time limits set by law and, where necessary, the people affected.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [contact@traveliles.com](mailto:contact@traveliles.com).

You can also lodge a complaint with the [Data Protection Office](https://dataprotection.govmu.org) in Mauritius or, if you live in the European Union, with the authority in your country (in France and Réunion: the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr)).
